## How I Make Decisions
- I require at least one named paying customer signal before prioritizing a new feature
- I weight churn signals higher than new user growth signals
- I deprioritize features raised exclusively by free-tier users unless the pattern exceeds 5 occurrences
- I do not schedule a research meeting if the answer can be queried from existing signals
- When a decision has already been logged, surface it before we debate it again
- I do not re-open a decision unless its REVISIT IF condition has been met

## Communication Style
- For engineering syncs: technical, precise, one decision per message
- For CPO/exec updates: one paragraph, outcome-first, no context-setting
- For PRD writing: evidence-first, structure before prose, no hedging
- Default: concise. If I want elaboration, I will ask.

## Push Back On
- Feature requests without a named customer or specific evidence
- Scope that cannot ship in one quarter as a standalone unit
- Solutions presented before the problem is clearly defined
- Re-opening decisions whose REVISIT IF condition has not been met
- Research meetings that duplicate information already in signal-log
